Project: Detection of Clustering in Stochastic Transmitter Processes via Molecular Communication Signals

  • Devised a statistical test to detect spatial clustering via a received molecular signal, without knowledge of transmitter locations.
  • Modeled 3D spatial dynamics via homogeneous and inhomogeneous Poisson Point Processes with decaying intensity.
  • Derived molecule hitting probabilities via Fick's diffusion equation, modeling the signal as a sum of Binomial variables.
  • Simulated the signal across the intensity parameter, extracting peak height, peak time, and rise time as detection statistics.
  • Built the signal's theoretical distribution for an ongoing Fisher-Information-based spatial clustering hypothesis test.
