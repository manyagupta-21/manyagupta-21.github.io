Internship

Research Intern — Student Advance Research Internship Program, IIT Kanpur

May '26 – Jul '26

Mentor: Dr. Abhishek Kumar Gupta, Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT Kanpur

Project: Detection of Clustering in Stochastic Transmitter Processes via Molecular Communication Signals

  • Devised a statistical test for detecting clustering in transmitter arrangements using only the aggregate received signal Y(t), with no knowledge of individual transmitter locations.
  • Modeled transmitter spatial distributions via homogeneous and inhomogeneous 3D Poisson Point Processes around an absorbing spherical receiver.
  • Derived molecule hitting probabilities from Fick's diffusion equation; modeled Y(t) as a sum of independent Binomial random variables.
  • Simulated Y(t) across the decay parameter β, identifying peak height, peak time, and rise time as clustering indicators — laying groundwork for an ongoing Fisher-Information-based detection test, relevant to drug delivery and neuroscience.
